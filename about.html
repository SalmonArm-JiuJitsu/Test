About Us

Our Coaches

Shaun

Experience

Shaun brings over 14 years of experience in grappling and MMA.

Role

He leads all youth and adult programs, designing, refining, and optimizing training exercises and program structure to support student skill development.

Focus

His approach is rooted in a deep interest in how people learn, with an emphasis on practice design, adaptability, and developing effective, high-level jiu-jitsu. Shaun is constantly refining how sessions are structured to help students progress as efficiently and effectively as possible.

Jessi

Experience

Jessi brings over 16 years of experience in grappling and MMA.

Role

Our Club

We take our role as leaders seriously, but that doesn't mean creating a rigid or overly formal environment.

A comfortable space

For us, it's about running a gym where people feel comfortable, supported, and challenged at the same time.

That starts with a clean space, clear expectations, and a culture built on embracing challenges.

Training that holds up

A lot of our focus goes into how we design training. We want people to develop skills that actually hold up over time in real situations.

Our sessions are built to encourage problem-solving, adaptability, and active engagement, with guidance throughout.

Part of something

We also believe a good gym is one where people feel like they're part of something.

Communication is open, and we value input from our members, but it's our responsibility to maintain a positive, respectful, and focused environment.

At the end of the day

Our job is to take care of the space, the people, and the training so everyone who walks in has the opportunity to improve and enjoy the process.
