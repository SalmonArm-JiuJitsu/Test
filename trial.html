Your First Class

Safety first

Remove All Jewelry

Rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, and watches all come off before class.

Trim Nails

Keep fingernails and toenails short and smooth, so no one gets scratched.

Be Clean and Tidy

Arrive clean, in clean clothes, with long hair tied back.

What To Wear

Avoid

Unsuitable

  • Anything with zippers, buttons, or metal
  • Hoodies, pockets, or loose, baggy clothing
  • Jewelry of any kind
  • Revealing clothing
Works fine

Suitable

  • Fitted t-shirt
  • Shorts/pants with a drawstring waist, no zippers, buttons or pockets
  • Comfortable, stretchy athletic wear
  • Workout clothes
Best choice

Recommended

  • Long-sleeve rashguard, fitted, no zippers or loose fabric
  • Leggings, or spats
  • Grappling-specific shorts over leggings or spats

Don't have a rashguard or spats? Fitted gym clothes are fine, no need to buy anything new.

What you need

  • Water bottle
  • Gym clothes
  • Barefoot or in socks, no special shoes needed
