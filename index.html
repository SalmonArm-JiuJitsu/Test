Quick questions

Do I have to wait until February to start?

No. Winter season runs Feb 1 - Jun 30, but you can start right away with a free trial class. When you register for Winter season, your training in December and January is free.

How do I register?

Start by booking a free trial class. Once you've tried a class, reach out and we'll send you your registration link.

How many days can I train?

Jiu-Jitsu runs Monday to Friday and open mat is on Saturday and Sunday, so you can train up to 7 days a week. Pick the days that fit your schedule.
